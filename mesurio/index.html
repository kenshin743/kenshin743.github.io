Type it like you read it

5' 7 3/8" + 2' 4 1/2". The answer comes back in feet, inches and fractions, rounded to the 1/16 (or 1/2 to 1/64), ready to mark.

Areas, volumes, metric

12' × 10' × 4" gives square feet, cubic feet and cubic yards. One tap converts to meters, centimeters or millimeters.

Jobsite tools

Pitch and diagonal, stairs, concrete in yards and bags, even spacing for balusters and pickets.

No subscription

The calculator is free, with no ads and no account. Mesurio Pro is a one-time purchase: you pay once and keep it forever.
