Privacy Policy — Raftio

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · Version française

In short: Raftio collects no personal data at all. No account, no ads, no trackers, no analytics. Your calculations stay on your device.

1. Who we are

Raftio is a roof framing calculator app published by Weekom (Fathi Rouassi, France). Questions: rouassif74@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

None. The app works entirely offline:

  • Your dimensions and settings (units, last opened shape) are stored on your device only and are never sent to us.
  • PDF plans are generated locally and remain under your control; they never pass through any server.
  • Raftio requests no sensitive permission (no location, contacts, camera or microphone).

3. Raftio Pro purchase

The "Raftio Pro" unlock is an in-app purchase handled entirely by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play. We never receive or store any payment data; Apple and Google only tell us, anonymously, that the purchase happened. The receipt is kept by the platform, which is what makes restoring the purchase on your other devices possible.

4. Third-party services

Raftio embeds no advertising SDK, no audience measurement and no third-party analytics. The app's only network exchanges are those of the purchase platform (App Store / Google Play) when buying or restoring Pro.

5. Children

Raftio is a professional tool with no inappropriate content. Since it collects no data, it collects no children's data either.

6. Your rights

As we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to show, fix or delete. To wipe the local settings, simply uninstall the app. For any question about your rights (GDPR), write to rouassif74@gmail.com.

7. Changes

If a future version of the app ever changes these rules, this page will be updated before that version ships, with its revision date.
